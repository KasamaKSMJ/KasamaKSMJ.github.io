UX/UI Design

TTT SpaceShip

Project Overview

A game-inspired software management platform designed around IT processes to engage the younger generation, who value freedom, challenges, and competition over rigid rules and restrictions. Rather than dictating how tasks should be completed, the platform focuses on setting clear goals while giving users the freedom to choose their own approach. It serves as a flexible and efficient tool that supports comprehensive work management, enabling teams to manage their workflows effectively while encouraging creativity and friendly competition.

My Role

Role

UX/UI Designer

Focus

User experience and interface design

Deliverables

User flows, wireframes, and UI design

Design Process

1. Participated in meetings with executives and team members to analyze and discuss project requirements.
2. Created wireframes and UI mockups, and presented design concepts to team leads and executives.
3. Developed interactive prototypes to simulate the system and present its functionality to executives before development.
4. Coordinated and communicated with the development team to ensure the designs were properly implemented.
5. After development was completed, collaborated with the testing team to conduct initial system testing and verify functionality before presenting the system to executives.
6. Delivered the completed project to the executive team, marking the end of the project.
